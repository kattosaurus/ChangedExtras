## First, to Define Latex

A latex creature is a furry being made of **goo**. Colors vary: white, dark with masks, or colorful.

Latex begins as single eukaryotic cells. On human contact, they absorb a cell and split. The new cells absorb and replace every human cell until none remain. Some form bone structures. Others turn to pure goo after infecting a host.

Latex originated from attempts to cure Pale. Beware of Pale too, though if you're reading this, you likely survived it. It's a long-incubation virus that causes sickle-cell-like symptoms.

### Standard Latex Variants

* **White Latex:** Wolves (standard, pure, mutant bloodcell, knight, cerberus, fusion, custom), cat, kitsune, tiger, centaur, snake, yuin.
* **Dark Latex:** Wolves, partials, yufeng (normal/two-headed), benign orca and wolf, crow, phage wolves, hypno cat, raccoon.
* **Unclassified:** Alien, bee, dragons (blue, gold, red, yuin, cone), wolves (blue, keon), crocodile, deer, human, eel, fennec fox, medusa cat, plant, ming cat, moth, orca, otter, pink deer wyvern, purple fox, rabbit, red panda, shark, siamese cat, squirrel, stiger, clear lizard, watermelon cat, manta ray.

*Yes, the last list is the longest. We stopped trying to categorize them after the first year. They kept changing.*

## Organic Latex

There is another kind of latex creature you may not recognize: **organic latex**.

Unlike the creatures above, which are goo through and through, organic latex is made of **bone and flesh**, just like you (if you're human). The difference matters. Goo latex that forms bones is still goo at its core. Organic latex is flesh from the start.

### Organic Latex Variants

* Buny, Reyn, White mirror tiger, Beifeng, Crystal wolf and its horned variant, Dark dragon, Gas skunk and tiger, Gas wolves, Pool toy wolf, Sniper dog, White wolves.

## How to Protect Yourself Against Latex Creatures

Latex creatures are hard to kill, exceptionally fast, and highly durable. They actively pursue humanoids to assimilate. **However, they have weaknesses.**

### 1. The Luminara Bloom
We discovered a plant/fungi species that renders latex creatures passive. They refuse to attack unless provoked. It calms almost every variant, except those with exceptionally strong wills, such as **EXP-009** and **EXP-010**. Don't ask me about those two. Pray you never meet them. We haven't numbered past them yet, and I hope we never have to.

> **Tip:** Keep a Luminara bloom on your person at all times, along with a container of dirt to plant and cultivate it.

### 2. Perimeter Traps
If you can't find a Luminara bloom, dig a **2-block-thick trapdoor ring** around your safehouse or entryway. Even the smarter latex variants fail to navigate this logic trap.

### 3. Weaponry & Combat
The **TSC** developed specialized countermeasures: the baton and staff. Latexes are highly vulnerable to high-voltage electricity.

If you're unequipped, do not engage. **Dig down and hold completely still.** Running is a last resort, because they are faster than you.

## A Peculiar Experience

During one breach, I hid in a pit beneath the lab floor while a white wolf stalked the room above me. It stood over the opening for what felt like an hour. I held my breath until my vision blurred. It never looked down. Latexes hunt by movement, not by smell or sound. That's why digging down and staying still works.

## Reversing Transfur: Litix-Camonia

We found a way to reverse transfur. We aren't entirely sure how it works, but we know that it **works**. It is tedious to produce, however.

### Crafting Recipe

1. **Impure Ammonia (Base Ingredient)**
   * Arrange meat on the top, bottom, and middle-left slots.
   * Place flowers or plants in the four corners.
   * Place any seed in the center slot.
   * Place bone meal in the middle-right slot.

2. **Ammonia Particles**
   * Process the Impure Ammonia in the **Catalyzer** to break it down into pure Ammonia Particles.

3. **Compressed Ammonia**
   * Combine Ammonia Particles in an outer ring with a Biomass core.
   * *Biomass Formula:* Raw meat in a cross pattern, sticky substance in the corners, and latex base in the center.

4. **Ammonium Chloride**
   * Run the Compressed Ammonia back through the **Catalyzer**.

5. **Final Step: Litix-Camonia Solution**
   * Combine the **Ammonium Chloride** with an **Anti-Latex Base** in the **Unifuser**.
   * *Anti-Latex Base:* 2 Ammonia Particles + 2 Luminara Bloom Petals + 1 Latex Base.
