## First, to define latex

A latex creature is a **goo** furry being. Colors vary: white, dark with masks, or colorful.

Latex begins as single eukaryotic cells. On human contact, they absorb a cell and split.

These new cells absorb and replace all human cells until none remain.

Some form bone structures. Others turn into pure goo after host infection.

Latex came from trying to cure Pale. Beware of Pale too, though you likely survived it.

Pale is a long-incubation virus causing sickle-cell-like symptoms.
